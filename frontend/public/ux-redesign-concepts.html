UX Redesign Direction

Turn the bot into a Viral Post Studio, not just a generator.

The strongest product direction is an operator workflow: discover a story, decide if it is worth posting, generate the creative, review risk, then schedule or publish. The UI should make every step feel controlled, fast, and trustworthy.

Professional name: Viral Post Studio Primary screen: Review Queue Key metric: Publish Readiness Tone: editorial operations

Design Principles

1

Make the next action obvious

Separate Discover, Generate, Review, and Publish so users always know what to do next.

2

Show confidence before posting

Put quality, factual risk, duplicate risk, and hashtag checks beside the preview.

3

Design for repeated daily use

Cleaner spacing, fewer neon effects, durable typography, and dense but readable controls.

Concept A: Content Command Center

Recommended for product-market fit: a daily workspace for creators, agencies, and social operators.

Three Build Directions

1. Review Queue First

Recommended. Makes the app feel professional immediately: scan stories, rank candidates, preview posts, approve or reject.

2. Creative Studio First

Best for manual creators. Focus on custom post creation, carousel editing, caption refinement, and image regeneration.

3. Autopilot First

Best for automation buyers. Focus on rules, schedule, brand voice, safety checks, and weekly performance reporting.
